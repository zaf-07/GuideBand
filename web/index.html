What it does

See. Remember. Act.

Today’s vision apps make you aim a phone and tap buttons — then forget everything the moment you close them. GuideBand works the other way round.

See

Live hazard warnings spoken as you move: “Chair ahead, left.” Detection runs locally, so it stays fast and private.

Remember

Ask later: “Where did I leave my keys?” or “What did that sign say?” GuideBand keeps a private memory of what it saw.

Act

Say “Buy me a coffee.” It reads the item and price aloud, then pays only after you say yes.

Hands-free by design

The bracelet listens and speaks, the phone stays clipped to your chest. Your hands stay free for your cane, your dog, your day.

Bridge Coming soon

A bridge between deaf and blind people: sign language voiced aloud, and speech shown as large captions.

Made for AI wearables

GuideBand is an accessibility layer that plugs into AI wearables — starting with the Vexo bracelet.

How it works

The bracelet is your voice. The phone is your eyes.

Safety-critical detection runs locally and privately. Reasoning and natural voice use the cloud.

  1. 1

    You talk

    Speak to the Vexo bracelet on your wrist. A chest-clipped phone camera watches the way ahead.

  2. 2

    It understands

    Hazards are spotted on-device in real time. An AI agent handles questions, memory and requests.

  3. 3

    It answers — or asks

    You hear a clear spoken reply. For anything that matters, like paying, it waits for your “yes”.

Built for trust

Nothing happens behind your back.

An assistant you can’t see has to earn your confidence. GuideBand is designed around three promises.

  • Private by default

    No raw video or audio is stored.

  • You confirm

    Money never moves without a spoken yes.

  • Honest about doubt

    When it isn’t sure, it says so instead of guessing.

Please note: GuideBand is a prototype. It does not replace a white cane, guide dog, orientation training or emergency services.
