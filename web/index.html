What it does

Everything you need, one sentence away.

No menus to learn. Say what you want in your own words and GuideBand handles the steps.

Plan your day

Set reminders and timers by voice. GuideBand speaks up when it’s time — you never have to check a screen.

Journey mode

Walking directions read aloud, step by step. Route instructions stay clearly separate from camera notices.

Calls and messages

Call a contact or open WhatsApp with a sentence. Nothing is dialed or sent until you say yes.

Guardian sharing

Let a trusted person follow your trip until you arrive. Always your choice, always visible, off in one tap.

Bridge mode

Large live captions and phrase-to-speech make conversations easier with anyone, anywhere.

Phone and Vexo Band

Works with just your phone today. Pair a Vexo Band and your assistant moves to your wrist.

How it works

Three steps. Every time.

The same simple rhythm for every task, so you always know what happens next.

  1. 1

    Say it

    Say “Guide, I’m ready” or hold the talk button, then speak naturally.

  2. 2

    Hear it back

    GuideBand repeats what it understood and explains exactly what it is about to do.

  3. 3

    Confirm

    Say “yes” to continue or “not now” to cancel. You stay in control.

Built for trust

Nothing happens behind your back.

An assistant you can’t see has to earn your confidence. GuideBand is designed around three promises.

  • Hear every action

    Everything is spoken aloud before it happens.

  • You confirm

    Calls, messages and sharing wait for a clear yes.

  • Stop anytime

    One phrase or one tap ends listening and sharing.

Please note: GuideBand is a prototype. It does not replace a white cane, guide dog, mobility training or emergency services.
